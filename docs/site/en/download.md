## Purchase FAQ {#purchase-faq}

A quick guide to compatibility, the Trial, and usage conditions. See the [BOOTH product page](https://mametarovv.booth.pm/items/8754692) for current pricing and downloads, and the [software license agreement](./terms.md) for the governing terms.

### Which computers are supported? {#faq-platform}

HarmoFlow is Windows-only and requires a Vulkan 1.2-compatible GPU and driver. CPU, RAM, and VRAM figures are provisional guidance, not a guarantee for every configuration. Read the [requirements table](#requirements-title) and its notes, then use the Trial to check your own models and intended workload.

### How do the Trial and Full editions differ? {#faq-trial}

The Trial supports editing and saving projects, but texture export is disabled. Trial-saved projects can be opened in the Full edition. Full and Trial install as separate apps and keep separate preferences and material caches. Follow [installation and first launch](./help/start.md#app-install) to get started.

### Do I need a constant internet connection? {#faq-offline}

You need a connection to download the app and when first using downloadable image materials. Downloaded materials can be used offline; applied images are also copied into the project. Browsing the catalog, using procedural materials, and using manually imported images do not require a material download. An unsuccessful update check does not prevent startup. See [material downloads and offline use](./help/materials.md#material-downloads).

### Who owns the textures and images I create? {#faq-output-rights}

Section 3 of the EULA states that you retain rights to the textures, images, project data, and exports you create with HarmoFlow. If they contain third-party works or licensed assets, you must also follow those assets' conditions. Buying the app does not itself grant rights to models or materials you import.

### Can I sell works made with the bundled CC0 materials? {#faq-cc0-materials}

The bundled CC0 materials can be used commercially, including in works you sell. See the [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Trademark, publicity, privacy, and similar rights may still need separate consideration.

Other materials you import remain subject to their own licenses. HarmoFlow itself is governed separately by the [software license agreement](./terms.md), rather than the materials' CC0 terms.

### Can I use one license on multiple PCs? {#faq-multiple-pcs}

As a rule, each person needs one license. The same purchaser may use HarmoFlow on multiple PCs.

### Are team licenses available? {#faq-team-license}

Team licenses are currently under consideration. If you're interested, contact the publisher via Contact on the [Mametaro-an shop](https://mametarovv.booth.pm/) to discuss your needs.

### Can I give the app or installer to someone else? {#faq-redistribution}

The EULA prohibits redistribution, lending, and similar sharing of the software and installer. For the bundled CC0 materials, see [material usage conditions](#faq-cc0-materials). One license is generally intended for the purchaser alone, and the right to use the software is non-transferable. Ask the publisher before purchasing if your intended use needs clarification.

### What about updates and support? {#faq-support}

Check the BOOTH product page for current release information and sales conditions. Section 5 of the EULA does not oblige the publisher to provide updates, new features, support, or an operation guarantee unless separately stated. Before updating, follow the [backup and update steps](./help/start.md#update-backup). Contact the publisher through the HarmoFlow BOOTH product inquiry option or Contact on the [Mametaro-an shop](https://mametarovv.booth.pm/).
