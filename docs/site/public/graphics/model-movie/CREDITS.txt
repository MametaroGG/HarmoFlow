HarmoFlow website: layered painting and street-art decal concept movie

3D models and original color/normal textures: user-supplied quadrocket(1).glb and rocketlaucher(1).glb.
The supplied files contain no embedded author or license statement. No CC0 or other reuse license is asserted here.
Additional street-art motifs were generated for this website and combined with the existing HarmoFlow brand mark and lettering.
Varied painted panels and wraps are applied to the models' UV color textures, with chips, scratches and existing grime carried across the artwork.

Rendering and animation: white model, base coat, color blocking, details, decals, and completed lighting/composite.
The supplied tangent-space normal maps are interpreted as non-color data and introduced through detail and finishing stages.
NPR and PBR movies are explanatory renders, not recordings of HarmoFlow or claims of identical renderer output.
Original model files are not distributed in this website.
